# Portfolio Scope

## Purpose

This repository is a public-facing engineering case study and executable demonstration of a governed multi-agent retail decision-support architecture.

## Included

The portfolio includes:

- representative five-agent workflow design;
- synthetic retail data;
- simplified, explainable agent logic;
- governance and human-review patterns;
- automated tests;
- architectural documentation;
- CI configuration.

## Excluded

This repository does not include:

- research participant data;
- field-study records;
- retailer identifiers;
- credentials, access keys or passphrases;
- production configuration;
- confidential deployment material;
- unpublished participant findings;
- the complete doctoral research implementation.

## Relationship to Research

The portfolio reflects the engineering concepts and architecture of a broader doctoral research system while remaining independently reviewable and safe to share as a professional software-engineering artefact.
